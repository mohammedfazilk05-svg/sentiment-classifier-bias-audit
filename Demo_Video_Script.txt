DEMO VIDEO SCRIPT (about 60–90 seconds)

1. Introduction: This project audits a sentiment classifier for performance differences across subgroups.
2. Problem: Aggregate accuracy alone may hide differences in false-positive or false-negative rates.
3. Workflow: The notebook creates a synthetic dataset, uses TF–IDF and Logistic Regression, and evaluates a held-out test set.
4. Metrics: It reports accuracy, precision, recall, F1, FPR, FNR, and positive prediction rate.
5. Audit: Results are grouped by illustrative gender and writing-style categories. Configurable thresholds flag gaps for review.
6. Interpretation: Flags are screening prompts, not proof of discrimination. Small groups and synthetic data limit conclusions.
7. Close: The notebook, report, output screenshots, and script are included. The project can be uploaded to GitHub for submission.
